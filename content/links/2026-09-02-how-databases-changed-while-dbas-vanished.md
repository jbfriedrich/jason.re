> Year 2000:
> 
> Software developers: ~639,000
> Database administrators: 108,000
> Ratio: 6 developers per DBA
> 
> May 2023:
> 
> Software developers: 1,656,880
> Traditional DBAs: 76,140
> Ratio: 22 developers per DBA
>
> Software developer employment increased 159% over 23 years, while traditional DBA roles actually declined by 29%.
> 
> This isn’t just a statistic — it’s a fundamental shift in how software gets built. That 6:1 ratio in 2000 meant you probably had an Albert within shouting distance. Today’s 22:1 ratio means entire teams ship code without ever talking to a database specialist.
