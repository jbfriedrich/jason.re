The OVH datacenter fire exposed serious gaps in operations and failover design: no sprinklers, wooden ceilings, generators that kept the building powered while it burned. This piece is from 2024, when the fire was three years old and there was still no official explanation. OVH had promised a report and never wrote one, the class action went nowhere, and customers who won in court were stuck in appeals.

> Three years on, there is no official story of its final hours. OVHcloud has cleared away the rubble, built a new data center on the site, and ignored any requests for information about it.

> The secrecy will come as no surprise to data center people. Covering up failures is normal practice and, as industry observers have commented time and again, it is supremely bad practice - because it means the same thing will very likely happen again.
