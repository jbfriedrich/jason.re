> Recently I reblogged something where I just thought “that's a pretty imaginary place”, and I thought nothing more of it until someone yelled in my comments “ThIs is unfortunately LLM trash. Check the carpet.” and I was just like... who cares? Looking at it you knew the image wasn't real. You knew it was generated, and all it was was someone sharing a comfy place that they'd created with the help of generative AI. The kinda image that before just wouldn't have existed unless the person also had the artistic skills and inclination to make it, or the money to hire people to make it look like a reality, something you definitely couldn't have afforded.
