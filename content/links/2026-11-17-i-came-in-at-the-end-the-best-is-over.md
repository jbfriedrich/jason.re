Brandon reflects on a feeling that the best of his own era has passed. He misses a “smaller” world with less constant connection and slower entertainment. He argues that technology has shifted from serving people to demanding their constant attention. 

I agree with a lot of things he wrote. Also with the feeling that it might be more of a nostalgic longing for one’s younger self, with fewer responsibilities and more possibilities, than about the era itself.
