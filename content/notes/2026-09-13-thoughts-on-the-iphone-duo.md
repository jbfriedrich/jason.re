The iPhone Duo appeals to me somehow. I always wanted to have one of those „[Westworld](https://cdn.frdr.ch/westworld-phablet1.webm) [Phablets](https://cdn.frdr.ch/westworld-phablet2.webm)“, and this is as close as it gets. It would replace my iPad as well – which is just used for watching videos and reading longer texts these days. But the responsible adult in the back of my head really cannot justify spending at least 2.299€ for a mobile device.

I also have to consider that this is a „1.0“ version, which will have a lot of teething problems. Sure, AppleCare+ will ease, and maybe even resolve most of these issues, but that would cost another 210€ per year. That is quite  an early adopters fee. In addition to that: Most of the iOS features that would benefit from an iPhone 18 Pro Max or an iPhone Duo, will not be released in the EU in the foreseeable future – maybe never.

I think I pass. But I will definitely try one out in an Apple Store.
