> I know that sounds simple. Maybe even naive. But good people have flaws. It can be hard to see the humanity in people whose politics feel so foreign to yours, or imagine bonding with those who excuse or, worse, endorse behavior that’s antithetical to yours. But those same people can be loving parents, generous neighbors, and devoted friends. The reverse is true as well. People with the “right” politics can have serious personal failings. If we demand perfection from everyone, we end up alone. And loneliness? That’s what’s really killing us. — [Jessica Tarlov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Tarlov)
