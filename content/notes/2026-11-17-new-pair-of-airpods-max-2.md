While visiting the Apple Store to take a closer look at the new iPhone Duo, I took the opportunity to test and eventually buy a pair of AirPods Max 2. The blue color was the right choice for me, I absolutely love it. The sound is great, they are comfortable for my head and the pairing with Apple devices, too (as expected). Still have to test the pairing with non-Apple devices.

Something I definitely do not like is the lack of a power button. If I do not put them back into their „case“ (calling it a case really is a euphemism), they will go into a „low power mode“ after 5 minutes and into a „ultra low power mode“ after 72 hours without use. If I put them into their „case“, they immediately go into „low power mode“, and after 18 hours into „ultra low power mode“. I’d rather have a fucking power switch…
