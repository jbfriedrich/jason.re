> Maybe thats the actual secret to a long creative life. Not protecting your opinions. Protecting your curiosity.
> 
> I hope when I’m eighty I’m still walking into a gallery, looking at something I dont get, and my first thought isnt that this isnt art. I hope its closer to, I havent seen that before.
> 
> Because thats the real difference. Between the ones who keep growing their whole lives and the ones who quietly stop. The ones who stop decided the world was beautiful once, back when they were young, and hasnt been since. Thats not true. Somewhere right now some twelve year old is falling in love with something for the first time, and its going to mean everything to them.
> 
> I’d rather stay curious.
